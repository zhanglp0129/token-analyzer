# Token Scoring Rubric

Each item is scored from 0 to 5 points. Total 20 items, max 100 points.
"Hard flaws" can result in negative scores for specific items, but the total score is floored at 0.

## 1. Tokenomics (Max 30 pts)
1. **Circulation Rate**: >30% (5 pts), 10%-30% (3-4 pts), <10% (0-2 pts, risk).
2. **Allocation Balance**: Team/Institutions vs. Public. High concentration = high manipulation risk (0-2 pts).
3. **Holding Concentration**: Top 50 addresses (excluding CEX/Contracts). High concentration = dump risk (0-2 pts).
4. **Retention Incentives**: Buyback/Burn mechanisms, staking rewards, lock-ups (0-5 pts).
5. **Inflation/Deflation**: Deflationary or capped supply (5 pts), predictable inflation (3-4 pts), unlimited inflation (0-1 pts).
6. **Utility**: Governance, staking, gas, payments, etc. (0-5 pts).

## 2. Team & Funding (Max 15 pts)
1. **Team Background**: Doxxed, elite education (Ivy League/Tsinghua/etc.), top-tier experience (FAANG/Big Tech/Tier 1 Crypto) (0-5 pts).
2. **Institutional Backing**: Tier 1 VCs (a16z, Paradigm, Polychain, etc.) (0-5 pts).
3. **Funding Valuation**: Reasonable FDV vs. TVL/Revenue. Avoid high FDV/Low float traps (0-5 pts).

## 3. Market Performance (Max 10 pts)
1. **Exchange Listings**: Tier 1 (Binance, OKX, Coinbase) (5 pts), Tier 2 (Gate, Bybit, Kucoin) (3-4 pts), DEX only (0-2 pts).
2. **Liquidity**: Depth, slippage, volume. 2% depth relative to volume (0-5 pts).

## 4. Tech & Code (Max 10 pts)
1. **Open Source & Activity**: Github activity, commit frequency, code quality (0-5 pts).
2. **Security Audits**: Audited by top firms (CertiK, OpenZeppelin, Trail of Bits) (0-5 pts).

## 5. Community & Sentiment (Max 15 pts)
1. **Engagement**: X followers, active Discord/Telegram, governance participation (0-5 pts).
2. **Market Sentiment**: Social signal, major risk news (0-5 pts).
3. **Operations**: Long-term consistency in updates and marketing (0-5 pts).

## 6. On-chain Data (Max 10 pts)
1. **Address Activity**: Active addresses growth, net inflows (0-5 pts).
2. **Whale Behavior**: Net flow of large holders. Accumulation (5 pts), Distribution (-5 to 0 pts).

## 7. Competitive Advantage (Max 10 pts)
1. **Market Positioning**: Advantage over competitors (0-5 pts).
2. **Innovation**: Technical moats, patents, unique mechanisms (0-5 pts).

---
**Hard Flaws (Negative Points Allowed for these items):**
- Honeypot/Malicious contract: -100 (Instant 0)
- Hidden minting/No ownership renounced: -20 to -50
- Fake social engagement: -10 to -20
- Plagiarized code/whitepaper: -20 to -40
- Team members with fraud history: -50
